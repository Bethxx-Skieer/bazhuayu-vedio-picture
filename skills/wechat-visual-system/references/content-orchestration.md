# Content orchestration

Use this reference when the user provides a topic, article, draft, product description, or mixed source material and expects a complete WeChat visual set.

## Article input → one confirmation → complete set

When given an article, infer the content type, central claim, audience and available real assets. Recommend one visual direction using these cues:

| Article content | Likely visual direction |
|---|---|
| Customer case or person story | Real person plus a supported outcome or viewpoint; header may add an introduction |
| Opinion or trend analysis | Title-led composition |
| Product feature or solution | Title plus a real product interface or evidenced business scene |
| Tutorial or method | Concise topic plus a clear content label; real screenshot when useful |
| Event or company visit | Event theme plus verified speaker/company, time and place as appropriate to the purpose |

Co-branding is an optional layer across these types, not an automatic requirement or a separate article genre. Do not invent a person or interface to satisfy a layout.

Present a compact confirmation card: central claim; recommended visual type and reason; exact main/header, blog and short square copy; real assets; registered theme; output purposes and dimensions. Give one alternative only when a real tradeoff warrants it. For a full set, list main cover 1800×766, article header 1800×766, blog 900×540, square 766×766 and main-plus-square 2566×766.

Wait for direction confirmation before rendering a new article-led set, unless the user has approved the direction or explicitly asked to proceed. Once confirmed, produce all agreed outputs without repeated per-size confirmation. Honor narrower requests and approved revisions directly. Ask again only for a material change to the claim, missing essential evidence or scope.

### Extract the visual message

Identify:

1. subject: product, method, event, customer, or result;
2. primary value: what changed or why the reader should care;
3. proof: real screenshot, data, workflow, logo set, or concrete scenario;
4. audience: operator, manager, developer, customer, or general reader;
5. tone: announcement, tutorial, case study, explanation, or opinion.

Do not turn every source sentence into cover copy. Prefer one clear promise or conclusion.

## Derive copy by format

### Main cover

- Communicate the topic and primary value.
- Prefer one dominant headline plus at most one support line.
- Select text-only when wording is the strongest asset.
- Select left-copy/right-screenshot when a real screenshot provides useful evidence.
- Select the protected version when the WeChat overlay title will be white.
- Keep designed copy and essential evidence out of the actual platform-title area. Reduce supporting information as needed; removing logos or moving copy upward is an option, not a blanket rule.

### Blog cover

- Distill the same topic into a short, immediately legible label or conclusion for the `900 × 540` canvas.
- Prefer one centered line for a short topic; use at most two lines when the meaning cannot remain intact on one line.
- Increase title scale and tracking only within the registered safe area. Do not insert arbitrary spaces into Chinese copy to simulate tracking.
- Recompose it separately from the main cover. Preserve the topic and theme, but do not mechanically copy the main-cover line breaks or geometry.
- Include it as a regular paired output when producing a complete cover set unless the user explicitly requests fewer formats.

### Customer-case person cover

- Use only when a real supplied customer or speaker photo provides meaningful story evidence.
- Default the category line to `标杆客户案例`.
- Prefer a concise two-line headline that states the change, result, or management shift. A before/after structure such as `从「旧状态」` / `到「新状态」` is appropriate only when supported by the source material.
- Put the photo on the side opposite the subject's gaze or gesture and the copy in the direction of attention. Do not flip real evidence to manufacture that direction.
- Keep the copy group centered within its side of the canvas and left-align every line.
- Omit the explanatory subtitle by default. The real scene is the proof; the headline should carry the message without a third text level.
- Use the selected theme's normal text color for structural words and its title gradient for the core contrast phrase. Do not introduce a separate palette.
- If the case label, customer name, claim, or outcome is not confirmed, ask or omit it. Never invent a customer endorsement or performance result.

### Secondary cover

- Distill the topic into a short category, content type, or reader benefit.
- Prefer four to six Chinese characters when natural, such as `客户案例`, `解决方案`, or `实操教程`.
- Do not mechanically repeat the full main-cover headline.
- Keep manual line breaks balanced and semantic.

### Article header / 公众号正文头图

- Default to `1800 × 766`, independently composed from the same-sized main cover.
- There is no platform-title overlay inside the article. Use the full canvas with normal margins for a richer hierarchy: approved co-brand logos, full theme, person and introduction, subtitle, or useful real screenshot.
- Include only the elements justified by the article and confirmed direction; richer does not mean mandatory extra copy.
- Choose centered type, directional person/copy or screenshot-led layout from the article's evidence. Do not carry over the main cover's bottom reservation or title-protection scrim.
- `1920 × 1080` is an optional article landscape variant when requested, not the default definition of a header.

### Real-task example: 芸易达 customer case

The main cover needed its co-brand logos removed and its designed title moved upward because WeChat's article title obscured the lower content. The article header could keep the co-brand logos, two-line theme, right-edge person and introduction bar. Both can be 1800×766. Reuse this purpose distinction, not the particular logo removal, title coordinates or decorative icons. If a supplied person's body is cropped at one edge, align that edge with the canvas boundary rather than exposing an abrupt cut inside the image.

### Main-plus-secondary composite

- Join the finished `1800 × 766` main cover and `766 × 766` secondary cover directly left-to-right.
- Do not rescale either image.
- Do not insert a seam, gap, white edge, or extra outer margin unless explicitly requested.

## Editorial judgment

- Use judgment when wording lengths differ.
- Rewrite for clarity and visual fit while preserving the user’s factual meaning.
- Shrink only the overflowing fixed text slot after concise rewriting is exhausted.
- Choose one emphasized phrase; do not scatter emphasis across unrelated words.
- Ask only when a decision would change factual claims, brand naming, confidentiality, or the intended audience.
- Never invent performance numbers, customer claims, product screens, endorsements, or outcomes.

## Missing screenshots

Article body illustrations are normally based on real screenshots supplied by the user.

- Generate the cover set first when sufficient topic content exists.
- Record the missing screenshot requirement without blocking unrelated outputs.
- After screenshots arrive, frame them using the same selected theme and the rules in `production-rules.md`.
