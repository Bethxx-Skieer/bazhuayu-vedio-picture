# Theme recipes

Use theme IDs exactly as registered below. Names describe the gradient itself and never reference another product or brand.

## Registered themes

### `dark-spectrum`

- Character: black background with blue–violet–coral gradient type.
- Background: `#000000`.
- Title gradient: `#2e80ee → #826db3 → #f06e68`.
- Body text: `#f5f7fb`.
- Emphasis: `#3685e9`.
- Screenshot border: soft cool white.
- Best for: technology announcements and strong centered titles.

### `dark-blue-purple`

- Character: near-black background with blue–purple gradient type.
- Background: `#02050c → #060a18 → #090a21`.
- Title gradient: `#4da7ff → #6f91ff → #a578ff`.
- Body text: `#eef2ff`.
- Emphasis gradient: same blue–purple family.
- Screenshot border and shadow: restrained blue–purple.
- Best for: premium AI and product capability stories.

### `dark-blue-aqua`

- Character: black background with blue–cyan–green gradient type.
- Background: `#000000`.
- Title gradient: `#148cff → #12cbed → #43db9d`.
- Body text: `#efffff`.
- Emphasis gradient: `#149dff → #11d2d4 → #43dc98`.
- Screenshot border and shadow: restrained cyan.
- Best for: automation, activation, flow, and efficiency.

### `light-blue`

- Character: light blue background with dark-blue title and clean white surfaces.
- Background: `#cfe1ff → #eaf4ff → #d9ebff`.
- Title gradient: `#102457 → #164fbd → #1267ef`.
- Body text: `#263b66`.
- Emphasis: `#0865f5`.
- Screenshot border: pale blue-gray.
- Best for: explanatory, educational, and article-oriented visuals.
- Do not add a grid unless explicitly requested.

### `deep-blue`

- Character: saturated blue background with white title and yellow emphasis.
- Background: `#316bd3 → #3479df → #58a2f3`.
- Title: white to ice-blue.
- Body text: `#eef5ff`.
- Emphasis: `#ffed6b`.
- Screenshot border: translucent white.
- Best for: official product releases and energetic feature stories.

### `background-blue-purple`

- Character: full-frame blue–purple gradient.
- Background: `#318ce9 → #5f72e8 → #9b69df`.
- Title: white to pale violet.
- Body text: `#f4f6ff`.
- Emphasis: `#ffed72`.
- Screenshot border: translucent white.
- Best for: vivid hero graphics with a large white interface screenshot.

### `background-blue-aqua`

- Character: full-frame blue–cyan–green gradient.
- Background: `#168cec → #10bdd2 → #45d59c`.
- Title: white to pale mint.
- Body text: `#efffff`.
- Emphasis: `#ffed72`.
- Screenshot border: translucent white.
- Best for: workflow, automation, and “start now” messages.

## Selection rules

- Treat palette changes as recipes, not new layouts.
- Keep title, support text, screenshot border, and shadow contrast coordinated.
- Do not mix two theme recipes in one image.
- Prefer `light-blue` for dense explanatory content.
- Prefer the three dark themes for title-led visuals.
- Prefer the two full-background gradients when the screenshot is predominantly white.
