# Copy rules

## 1. Write for the page’s job

- Cover copy earns attention.
- Evidence copy names what the screenshot proves.
- Process copy makes order and action clear.
- Comparison copy creates recognition with concrete work.
- Feature copy helps readers scan and choose.
- Conversion copy tells readers what to do next without hard selling.

Do not ask one headline to carry click appeal, full mechanism, proof, disclaimer, and call to action.

## 2. Headline formula

Lead with a recognizable object, action, or outcome. Prefer:

- `对象 + 自动完成的动作` — “公众号文章，批量采集导出”
- `不用再做的重复动作` — “这些微信操作，不用手动重复做”
- `工具/机制 + 跑完的流程` — “AI + 机器人，自动跑完招聘流程”
- `具体痛点 + 替代方式` — “别再手动刷简历了，AI + 机器人自动跑招聘流程”
- `数量 + 场景 + 结果` — “4 步自动跑完招聘流程”

Keep a Xiaohongshu cover headline to roughly `12–20` characters when possible, split into `2–3` lines. Highlight one phrase only. Use punctuation sparingly. The cover headline and the platform note title are separate copy fields; do not assume one automatically fits the other.

For the platform note itself, keep the title within `20` characters and the body within `1000` characters, including punctuation, whitespace, line breaks, Emoji, hashtags, and any symbols that will actually be published. Prefer a body within `900` characters to preserve editing room. Count the title and body separately with a deterministic checker; never use the total character count of a Markdown file as a substitute.

Avoid:

- empty superlatives: “全网最强,” “颠覆式,” “史诗级”; 
- unverifiable outcomes: “效率提升 100 倍,” “彻底解放双手”; 
- ad voice: “震撼上线,” “错过后悔,” “立即抢购”; 
- vague AI stacking: “AI 赋能数智化转型新范式”; 
- title plus subtitle that repeat the same fact.

## 3. Process steps

Use parallel verb-object grammar. Keep each step short enough to scan.

Good:

1. 机器人跨平台找人
2. AI 自动筛选候选人
3. 机器人自动打招呼
4. 结果自动回填表格

Each step states actor or mechanism plus action. Do not mix an action, a benefit, a product name, and a disclaimer as equal steps.

## 4. Feature cards

Use two levels:

- feature name: recognizable product action;
- one-line explanation: scope, input, output, or supported format.

Example:

`微信—定时自动发朋友圈`

`支持文案、图片和图文混合发布`

Avoid repeating “支持” on every card when the feature name already communicates the action. Prefer concrete objects: contacts, groups, keywords, accounts, time range, Excel, or Feishu multi-dimensional table.

## 5. Screenshot annotations

An annotation should answer one of three questions:

- What action happened here?
- Which field or state matters?
- What output proves completion?

Use short labels such as “AI 判定不匹配,” “自动发送招呼,” or “结果已回填.” Point to a real UI region. Avoid narrative paragraphs on top of screenshots and avoid labels such as “看这里” without saying why it matters.

For a missing-image placeholder, write three separate fields:

- asset needed: “任务运行成功截图”;
- claim to prove: “任务按计划完成，并保留运行状态”;
- crop guidance: “保留任务名称、状态和运行时间.”

Do not write “效果图待补” or “放一张截图” because those instructions do not tell the producer what the image must establish.

## 6. Pain-point and comparison copy

Name observable repeated work:

- 手动筛简历
- 反复点开候选人
- 打招呼次数容易浪费
- 多群消息来回查看
- 文章逐篇复制到表格

On the improved side, show the replacement mechanism or result. Do not shame users or exaggerate their current process. Limit each side to about three burdens or benefits unless the mechanism itself is a numbered flow.

## 7. Pills, labels, and highlights

Use a pill only for:

- content category: “微信操作自动化”;
- state: “自动化后”;
- short value: “减少重复操作”;
- conversion entry: “搜索八爪鱼 RPA.”

Keep most pills within `4–10` Chinese characters. Do not place full explanatory sentences inside pills. Use color highlights for nouns, actions, or results that drive the page; do not color every keyword.

## 8. Official-brand voice

Sound knowledgeable, specific, and useful.

- State the function before praising it.
- Use real scenarios and objects.
- Keep confidence proportional to evidence.
- Include platform-rule or usage notes only when relevant and keep them visually secondary.
- Use the exact product and platform names consistently.
- Prefer “可… / 支持… / 用于…” for factual capability descriptions, but vary sentence openings to avoid catalogue monotony.

An official page may still have personality. Use a reader-language cover, then switch to precise product language on evidence and feature pages.

## 9. Calls to action

Use one restrained next step:

- search the product name;
- open the application link;
- view the workflow template;
- save the card for later;
- continue to the next evidence page.

Do not stack “点赞 + 收藏 + 评论 + 私信 + 立即体验” on an official product card. If the application link is not directly usable inside the image, name the search path or destination clearly.

## 10. Fact and compliance checks

Before finalizing, verify:

- every capability is supported by provided material;
- every number has a source;
- the screenshot and caption describe the same state;
- platform names and feature names are accurate;
- no personal data appears in screenshots;
- automation claims do not promise evasion of platform controls;
- disclaimers do not contradict the headline.

When evidence is incomplete, narrow the claim instead of filling the gap with promotional language.

## 11. Copy self-check

- Can the title be understood without the body?
- Does the title contain one main promise rather than a list?
- Are process steps parallel and specific?
- Does each annotation explain a meaningful action or result?
- Are pills and highlights scarce enough to remain useful?
- Does the voice sound like an experienced product team rather than an advertising generator?
- Can any sentence be removed without losing meaning? If yes, remove it.
